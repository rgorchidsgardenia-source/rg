FAQ

Commonly asked questions

Where is RG Orchids Gardenia located?

In Nagawara, Bengaluru, directly opposite Manyata Tech Park in North Bangalore.

What types of apartments are available?

Ultra-luxury 3 BHK apartments of 1,860 – 2,890 sq.ft, with only four homes per floor and three sides open.

What is the starting price?

Prices start from ₹2.79 Cr* onwards. Ask for the latest price sheet and the current 50:50 payment plan.

What is the total area of the project?

The project spans 2.7 acres with just 132 homes across three towers.

What is the UDS percentage?

RG Orchids Gardenia offers a high UDS (undivided share of land) of 38%.

What is the possession status?

The homes are ready for interiors possession. Talk to our team for the exact handover timeline of your unit.

What are the key amenities?

An 11,000 sq.ft clubhouse, swimming pool, gym, indoor games room, large home theatre, cafe zone, park, 24/7 CCTV surveillance and EV charging stations.

Are the homes automated?

Yes. Every home has full home automation with centralised air-conditioning, touch panels, sensor lighting and digital locks with a video door phone.

Is there power backup?

Yes, 24/7 power backup with 100% backup for every apartment.

What flooring is provided?

Italian marble in living and dining, vitrified tiles in bedrooms, and laminated wooden flooring in the master bedroom.

Do the apartments come with modular kitchens?

German modular kitchens are available at extra cost as part of the interior package.
