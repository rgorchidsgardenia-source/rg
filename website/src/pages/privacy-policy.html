Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information RG Unique Structures Pvt Ltd (“we”, “us”) collects through rgstructures.in and how it is used.

1. Information you give us

When you submit a form on this website (call-back requests, contact, channel partner registration, lead registration or job applications) we collect the details you enter, such as your name, phone number, email address, city, message and preferred visit date.

2. Information collected automatically

  • The page you submitted a form from, the website that referred you and campaign tags in the link (for example utm_source)
  • Anonymous usage statistics: pages viewed, device type and clicks on call, WhatsApp and direction buttons
  • Your IP address and browser type, used to prevent spam and abuse

3. How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and share information about our projects
  • To arrange site visits and follow up on your interest
  • To process channel partner registrations and job applications
  • To understand which pages and campaigns are useful, so we can improve the website

4. Sharing

We do not sell your information. It is stored with our hosting and database providers and is accessible only to authorised RG Unique Structures staff. The live chat on this website is provided by Tawk.to and is subject to its own privacy policy.
