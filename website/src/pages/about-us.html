Who are we

RG UNIQUE STRUCTURES, established in 2006, is a leading real estate developer with two decades of expertise, delivering 40+ projects spanning 30+ lakh sq. ft. Known for impeccable quality, technical excellence, and timely execution, the company prioritizes customer-centric development.

Under the leadership of Managing Director Mr. G.K. Rajagopal Naidu, RG UNIQUE STRUCTURES has expanded into a group of companies, offering modern residential and commercial spaces. Backed by a team of skilled professionals and top architects, the company is committed to creating fine living environments with unmatched value-for-money.
